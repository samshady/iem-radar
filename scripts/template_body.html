The Nozzle & Fit Reality Check

Why the sound doesn't matter if your ears start aching after 20 minutes.

Slim & Safe (5.3mm — 5.5mm)

Moondrop Chu II, 7Hz Zero:2, Truthear Gate, Tangzu Wan'er, Delci. Fits virtually everyone comfortably. Standard silicone ear tips slide on easily and seal without stretching your ear canal.

Medium / Standard (5.6mm — 5.8mm)

Kefine Klean, DUNU Titan X, Moondrop May, ooopusX Op.22. Secure, normal fit for adult ears. If you have particularly narrow canals, consider soft medical-grade tips like SpinFit CP100+ or DUNU Candy tips.

Wide Bore Caution (6.0mm — 6.2mm)

Truthear Zero:Red & KZ ZAR. The sound on Zero:Red is world-class reference tuning, but the physical 6.2mm brass stem is chunky. Small ear canals will feel uncomfortable pressure quickly.

How to Buy in Germany / EU Without Surprise Fees

Quick breakdown on AliExpress IOSS vs Amazon.de delivery.

AliExpress Choice & 11.11 Sales

Under EU rules (IOSS), AliExpress collects the German 19% MwSt automatically at checkout for orders under €150. Your package arrives straight to your door via DHL/Hermes in 6–10 days with zero customs fees or handling charges.

Amazon.de & Authorized Retailers

Amazon Germany prices are typically €6–€15 higher than AliExpress, but you get next-day Prime delivery and hassle-free 30-day returns—which is invaluable if you're unsure how an IEM will fit your ear shape.
