Anatomy & Nozzle Girth Alert: Don't Buy Blindly!

IEM nozzle diameter is the #1 cause of return rates and ear ache. Standard IEMs measure 5.2mm to 5.5mm. Models like the Truthear x Crinacle Zero:RED have a massive 6.2mm nozzle bore (6.8mm with outer lip) that causes physical ear-canal pain for small or medium ears after 30 minutes. If you have smaller ears, look for the 🌸 Small Ear Safe badge or choose ultra-slim flush models like the Moondrop Chu II or Kefine Delci.

SUB-BASS (20Hz - 60Hz)

The visceral physical rumble you feel rather than hear. Critical for cinematic EDM and modern pop. Dual-driver IEMs like Truthear Zero:BLUE 2 dedicate a dedicated 10mm dynamic driver just to this band.

MID-BASS (60Hz - 250Hz)

Punch, kick drums, and bass guitar notes. If elevated too much (>8dB above 300Hz), it bleeds into vocals and sounds "muddy". Controlled cleanly on 7Hz Zero:2 and Kefine Delci.

LOWER MIDS (250Hz - 1kHz)

Male vocals, acoustic body, and warmth. A clean "tuck" at 300Hz prevents boominess. Tangzu Wan'er 2 gives this range a rich, natural, intimate chest resonance.

PINNA GAIN / EAR GAIN (1kHz - 3kHz)

Natural amplification created by the human outer ear (concha). Harman targets boost this by ~9-11dB around 2.7kHz. If tuned wrong, female vocals sound either shouting or hollow. Perfected on Truthear Zero:RED.

PRESENCE (4kHz - 8kHz)

Detail articulation, consonant sounds (s, sh, t), and tactical gaming footstep cues. Sharp spikes here cause painful sibilance and ear fatigue. Balanced Armatures in Truthear Hexa keep this ultra-clean.

AIR & UPPER TREBLE (10kHz - 20kHz)

Room ambiance, cymbal decay, and soundstage extension. Planar magnetic drivers (like in Moondrop May) excel at reproducing this micro-detail without metallic harshness.

AliExpress IOSS (< €150)

AliExpress participates in the EU IOSS (Import One-Stop Shop). On orders under €150, German 19% MwSt is collected directly at checkout. The parcel clears customs in Frankfurt or Liège with zero additional fees and no Deutsche Post €6 collection charge.

11.11 & Choice Sales

The annual 11.11 Singles' Day (Nov 11–18) offers the deepest Chi-Fi discounts of the year (20%–35% off). Monthly Choice Day (1st–5th of each month) offers free 7–10 day Cainiao delivery to Germany with €3–€8 store coupons.

Amazon.de & Returns

Amazon.de carries a 15%–25% price premium over Chinese retail, but includes 30-day hassle-free returns. If you are trying an IEM with a wide nozzle (like the Zero:RED) or don't know your ear canal size, buying on Amazon.de is a risk-free way to audition the fit.
